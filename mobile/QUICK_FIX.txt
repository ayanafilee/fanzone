═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WHY THIS FIXES IT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Firebase Cloud Messaging does NOT automatically show notifications
when the app is in the foreground. You must manually display them.

flutter_local_notifications allows us to show notifications when
the app is open, completing the notification flow.
